Today we build a <Term id="rag">RAG</Term> pipeline over a company's policy handbook. We build it step by step,
on your own laptop. We open every intermediate output and look at it. The models run in Ollama, and the vector DB runs in Podman. The code is TypeScript.

  <Step title="Before we start">
    Setup and `npm run doctor`. Then the question of the day. First your guess, then the model's answer with no documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1 · Why RAG?">
    The model never saw our policy, but it still makes up numbers. There are two ways to give knowledge to a model: RAG and fine-tuning. Which one, and when?
  </Step>
  <Step title="2 · Architecture">
    The whole pipeline in one drawing. One lane prepares the documents once. The other lane runs on every question. The two lanes meet in the vector space. Every box is a file in the repo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="3 · Build it step by step">
    Eight steps run one by one. Each step writes its output to the `data/` folder. After each step, we open the file. The answer to the question of the day also changes, step by step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="4 · Ready for production?">
    "Looks good to me" is not a measurement. We put a number on quality with a set of questions. We test for security holes. We look at where the time and the tokens go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="5 · Does RAG fix everything?">
    Where does RAG fail? Which RAG variant fixes which failure? Which complaints come in during the first 90 days in production?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wrap-up">
    A design checklist for the whole pipeline: accuracy, security, performance and cost at every stage. All the answers to the question of the day, side by side.
  </Step>

## By the end of the day

Today is about RAG architectures. They help large language models use company data **safely and correctly**.
We cover data preparation, chunking, embedding, retrieval, rerank, answer generation and quality measurement. By the end of the day:

- You can **design a complete RAG pipeline** for company use. You run [every box of both lanes](/en/mimari/) yourself.
- You can prepare the data: [clean the raw documents](/en/adim-adim/a-ham-veri/) and [split them in the right places](/en/adim-adim/b-chunking/).
- You can show [what embedding finds close](/en/adim-adim/c-embedding/) and [what a vector DB is for](/en/adim-adim/d-vector-db/).
- You can read the results of [retrieval and rerank](/en/adim-adim/e-retrieval-rerank/) and [generate the answer with its source](/en/adim-adim/f-cevap/).
- You can judge key decisions on four points: **accuracy** ([Measuring quality](/en/uretim/kaliteyi-olcmek/)), **security** ([Security](/en/uretim/guvenlik/)), **performance** and **cost** ([Performance and cost](/en/uretim/performans-maliyet/)).
