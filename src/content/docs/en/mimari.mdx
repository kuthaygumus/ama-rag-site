  <Step title="The documents">
    Kraken Air has 8 documents in Markdown: 7 policies and 1 announcement, 2025 edition. Each one starts with information about itself: which document, which edition, who may read it. The top lane makes them searchable. It runs once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load, clean, split">
    `1-load.ts` reads the files and turns the information at the top into <Term id="metadata">metadata</Term>. `2-clean.ts` removes page noise and joins broken lines. `3-chunk.ts` splits the documents at their section headings into <Term id="chunk">chunks</Term>. The search finds chunks, not whole documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embed">
    `4-embed.ts` uses bge-m3 to turn each chunk into an <Term id="embedding">embedding</Term> of 1024 numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vector DB">
    `5-store.ts` writes each chunk to Chroma with its vector, its text and its metadata. Chroma is our <Term id="vectordb">vector DB</Term>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The question, with the same model">
    The bottom lane runs on every question. The question also becomes 1024 numbers, with the same model as the top lane. A different model means a different space, and you can't compare the two.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and rerank">
    `6-retrieve.ts` asks for the 5 chunks closest to the question. A metadata filter keeps old editions out of the search. It also keeps out documents the asker may not read. `7-rerank.ts` has the chat model score these 5 chunks, then sorts them again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prompt and answer">
    `8-answer.ts` puts the rules, the best 3 sources and the question into one text. The model cites the source by number: 22 working days, [1] Annual Leave Policy §3.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two lanes, two meeting points">
    The lanes meet in only two places: the vector DB and the same embedding model. The chat model works only in the bottom lane, for rerank and the answer.
  </Step>
</Deck>

<FileTree>
- src/
  - steps/ one file and one command per box (steps 1–8: table below)
    - 0-bare.ts step 0: the model alone, no documents
  - lib/
    - ollama.ts the two calls to the model: embed and generate
    - chroma.ts writes to Chroma and searches it
    - store-json.ts the same job, with a JSON file
    - config.ts every setting in one place
  - cli/
    - ingest.ts top lane: steps 1–5
    - ask.ts bottom lane: steps 6–8
    - question.ts the question of the day and its ledger
    - eval.ts measures quality
    - doctor.ts checks the setup
    - catchup.ts helps you catch up if you fall behind
  - explore/
    - similar.ts which texts are close?
    - map.ts the vector space, in 2 dimensions
- corpus/ Kraken Air policies, 2025 and 2026
- data/ each step writes its output here. Open it and look
</FileTree>
