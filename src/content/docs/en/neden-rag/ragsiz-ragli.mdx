  <Step title="Without RAG" stage="a">
    The question goes straight to the model. The answer is made up: 25-30 working days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search comes first" stage="a">
    With RAG, the question first becomes an <Term id="embedding">embedding</Term>. The <Term id="vectordb">vector DB</Term> returns the handbook <Term id="chunk">chunks</Term> that are closest in meaning. One of the three chosen chunks holds the leave table. For 7 years, it says 22 working days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prompt: sources + question" stage="a">
    The chosen chunks go in front of the question, together with the rules. We call the whole text that goes to the model the <Term id="prompt">prompt</Term>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Same model, right answer" stage="a">
    The model is the same. Nobody touched its weights. Only the text in front of it changed. The answer is 22 working days, with the source [1] hr-leave §3.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What if we put everything in the prompt?" stage="b">
    We put all 8 handbook documents into one prompt: 9954 tokens. The RAG prompt is 976 tokens. The answer is still 22 working days, but one call took 26337 ms, about 26 seconds. The whole RAG path, with search and <Term id="rerank">rerank</Term>, takes about 2 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The real job is choosing" stage="b">
    If you put everything in, you pay for the whole handbook on every question. As the corpus grows, one day it won't fit in the <Term id="context">context window</Term>. And everyone reads the salary table that only HR may see.
  </Step>

## Common questions

<FAQ q="What happens if the search brings the wrong chunk?">
The model works with the wrong context. It either says "I don't know" or writes a wrong answer. You can see a live
example on the [B · Chunking](/en/adim-adim/b-chunking/) page. When a document is split badly, the search doesn't
find the table. Then the model writes a wrong number, with a source. A source doesn't make an answer right.
</FAQ>

<Takeaway>The hard part is not giving the knowledge. It is choosing the right chunk.</Takeaway>
